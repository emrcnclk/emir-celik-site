GMRLOG has a North Star, and it is locked: a profile should answer *what kind of player is this?* the moment it opens. Everything in the first two weeks of August was measured against that sentence.

## The palette came first

The sprint opened by re-valuing the dark and light palettes and giving the app one plasma accent. Then a rule that sounds small and is not: **rarity is encoded as geometry, not colour alone.** A legendary badge has a different shape from a common one, so the information survives colour blindness, bad screens and screenshots.

The same pass turned up honest bugs. A toggle knob that "animated" but whose interpolation never advanced. A scrim that hard-coded black instead of reading its token. A cover that faked depth with a shadow when it should have been built from the surface. Each fix went in with the audit that found it.

## Screens, recomposed

From there it was screen by screen — Achievements, Settings, Notifications, Messages, Discover, Profile, Register, Onboarding, Circles — each rebuilt on shared pieces instead of local copies. Profile was recomposed around a *player record card*; Register and Onboarding moved onto one shared auth shell. Tier lists got drag-to-reorder. The review composer became a writing surface rather than a form.

## A match that shows its working

The feature I like most is DNA match. Two players get a similarity score — but the engine was changed to return **a breakdown, not just a score**, and the components are persisted. On a public profile the panel shows *why* you match, and it is gated to individual accounts. A number with no reasons is a horoscope.

## Signing in

Account matching first, then Google, then Discord, then Steam. Steam had a trap: a self-reported Steam ID could be used to squat someone else's account. That path was closed, and later refused outright outside development. Settings gained connect and disconnect, with a guard that will not let you remove your last way in.

The session closed with an accessibility pass on the DNA panel and its progress bars, and a full check: **33 of 33 turbo tasks green.**
